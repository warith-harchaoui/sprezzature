    Vision is part of Accessibility now

    On 17 October 2026 sprezzature-vision stopped being its own brick. Everything it did — drafting W3C-compliant alt text with a local vision model, the per-image decision tree, the on-disk cache — moved into sprezzature-accessibility.

    The reason is that they were two halves of one job and nobody wanted only one of them. The lint says a page is missing alt text; the drafter writes it. Keeping them apart meant two installs, two skills competing for the same prompts, and a drafter that could not see the page it was describing — which matters, because the W3C decision tree asks what an image is doing on the page, a question about the page and not about the pixels.

    This page stays so the link you followed does not break.
